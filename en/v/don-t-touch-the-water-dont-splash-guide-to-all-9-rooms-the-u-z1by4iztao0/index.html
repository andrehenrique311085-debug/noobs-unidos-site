Relax. Just don't touch the water.

Easy.

AAAH! But I calculated that!

Splash. And the monster heard it.

In DONT SPLASH, the monster is blind and hunts by the sound of the water.

And the last room has a locked exit. Stick around and I'll show you how to open it.

Now there are nine rooms.

I'll show you the path through each one, and the upgrades worth your coins.

Golden rule: touch the water, you make a splash. The monster goes to the noise. If it's close, you're done.

Room one: Pillar Hall.

Pillars in the middle of the water.

Aim for the center of each pillar, never the edge.

The arrows on the wall show the route. Landed on the edge?

You slip. Splash.

Room two: Broken Walkway.

A broken walkway: narrow ledges with short jumps, or wide platforms with long jumps.

One guide recommends the wide ones.

I agree: a big landing forgives mistakes.

Room three: Sinking Platforms.

They sink while you're standing on them.

The secret: only stop on the dark-edged ones, they stay firm.

On the light ones, keep jumping.

The mistake is stopping to think.

Room four: Rotating Bridge.

The bridge spins.

Wait for it to line up, get on and cross straight to the other end. Get on mid-spin and you're going swimming.

Room five, the first of the new rooms: Moving Platforms.

Platforms that move along fixed paths.

Watch one full loop of the pattern before you jump.

And don't pile the whole team on one platform.

Room six: Drifting Platforms.

They drift around on the water.

Jump to where the platform will be, not where it is.

And before you jump, check where the monster is.

Room seven: Memory Bridge.

The bridge lights up a sequence of tiles.

Memorize the sequence and repeat it.

Step on the wrong tile?

Water.

It was the blue one...

or the other blue one?

Room eight: Crushing Walls.

Walls that close in a rhythm.

Count the rhythm before going in.

One guide points to the mistake that kills the most here: entering the gap that's closing.

Room nine: Locked Exit.

The exit is locked.

Find the two red valves and hold E on each one.

Then the water rises: run up the yellow blocks to the exit.

Now, the shop.

Equipment is bought with coins, which you earn by clearing rooms and opening valves.

S tier: Rescue Rope.

It pulls a teammate back onto the platform from far away.

In a team, it saves the run.

A tier: Flare, four hundred fifty coins, stuns the monster for five seconds.

And Bottles, one hundred fifty: a loud splash far from you that pulls the monster over there.

Heads up: another guide puts Bottles in S tier. The sources don't agree.

B tier: Wind-Up Toy, the toy that makes noise for longer.

C tier: Pebbles.

You start with them, and five cost twenty-five coins.

Upgrades: there are six, each with three levels. Throwing Range gives ten percent more range per level.

Level one costs one hundred fifty coins.

What to buy first?

My take: solo, Bottles plus Throwing Range. In a team, the Rescue Rope.

Three quick tips.

One: throw a pebble into the water far from your route.

The monster chases the noise, and your path is clear.

Two: until October thirty-first, the Halloween pack gives double cash and one free auto-revive per match.

Use it.

Three: there are no active codes.
